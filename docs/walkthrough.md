# Reddify – App Walkthrough (A Story)

Nov 25, 2026 · @Saad Islam

This story follows one blood request in Dhaka from start to finish, showing every main feature of Reddify version 1 as the characters use it.

## The people in the story

| Person | Who they are | Blood group | Status at the start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rahim | His father needs surgery; not a Reddify user yet | — | No account |
| Nusrat | 24-year-old university student, lives in Badda | O+ | Not registered yet |
| Tanvir | Office worker in Bashundhara | B+ | Yellow (donated 40 days ago) |
| Mitu | Teacher in Rampura | B− | Green |
| Arif | Engineer in Mirpur | A+ | Green |
| Karim | Student in Uttara | B+ | Green |

Rahim's father, the patient, is B+ and is admitted to a hospital in Bashundhara, Dhaka.

## Scene 1: Nusrat registers as a donor

Nusrat installs Reddify after a friend shares it. The home screen shows a big red **Find Blood** button, an outlined **Register as Donor** button, and a small "Already have an account? Log in" link. She taps **Register as Donor**.

She is new, so she fills in everything: name, mobile number (01XXXXXXXXX), a password, blood group O+, gender, and date of birth. She is 24, inside the 18–60 range, so the app lets her continue. Had she been 17, the app would stop her and say she can still use Reddify to find blood.

For location she picks **Dhaka** and **Badda**. She skips the optional extra address field. The app asks for GPS permission; she allows it, knowing her exact location is never shown to anyone.

She last donated eight months ago, well past the 112-day gap for women, so she starts as **Green (Available)**. "Receive blood request alerts" is ON by default. Finally she reads the consent message: her name, blood group, area and availability are visible to Reddify users, and her number is visible only to people with an active blood request. She agrees and finishes.

Her dashboard now shows a green availability card, 1 donation in her history, and a shortcut to Open Requests in Dhaka.

## Scene 2: Rahim needs blood and signs up

At the hospital, the doctor tells Rahim his father needs 2 bags of B+ blood by tomorrow afternoon. A relative tells him about Reddify.

Rahim opens the app and taps **Find Blood**. He has no account, so the app asks for just three things: his name, mobile number and a password. Thirty seconds later he is logged in. He does not have to give any donor details, because he is only looking for blood.

He searches for **B+** in **Dhaka**, area **Bashundhara**. The results show names, blood groups, areas, gender and green or yellow availability dots, but **no phone numbers**. A banner explains: "Post a blood request to see donors' contact numbers."

## Scene 3: Rahim posts a request and alerts go out

Rahim taps **Post Request** and fills in: patient name (his father), blood group B+, 2 units, the hospital's name, district Dhaka, required date tomorrow, his own phone number, and a note: "Surgery at 2 PM, please come by noon." The app checks that the date is not in the past and that he has fewer than 3 open requests. The request goes live immediately as **Open**, with no approval step.

The moment it is posted, the server finds donors to alert. A B+ patient can receive B+, B−, O+ and O−, so it looks for **Green** donors in Dhaka with any of those groups and alerts switched ON:

- **Nusrat** (O+, Green): gets the alert.
- **Mitu** (B−, Green): gets the alert.
- **Karim** (B+, Green): gets the alert.
- **Tanvir** (B+, Yellow): no alert. He donated 40 days ago, and men must wait 84 days.
- **Arif** (A+): no alert. A+ cannot give to a B+ patient.

## Scene 4: Rahim finds and calls donors

Now that Rahim has an open request, he goes back to search. The filters are already set to B+ and Dhaka from his request. This time the donor cards show **Call** and **SMS** buttons.

The list is ordered for him:

1. Karim (B+, exact match, Green), with "5–10 km" because both of them allowed GPS.
2. Tanvir (B+, exact match), shown as Yellow with "Available in 44 days". Rahim can see him but knows he can't donate now.
3. Nusrat (O+) and Mitu (B−), labelled **Compatible**. Nusrat is listed with "2–5 km".

Arif (A+) does not appear, because his group isn't compatible. Rahim taps **Call** on Karim. Karim is in an exam hall and doesn't pick up. Rahim taps SMS and sends him a message.

## Scene 5: Nusrat taps I'm Interested

Nusrat's phone buzzes: "B+ blood needed in Bashundhara, 2 units, tomorrow." She taps the notification and sees the request details, including Rahim's phone number, because she is a donor.

She can make it tomorrow morning, so she taps **I'm Interested**. Three things happen:

- Rahim sees Nusrat listed under his request as an interested donor, with her number.
- Nusrat disappears from donor search for the next 3 hours, so other seekers don't call her while she is helping Rahim.
- She can't tap I'm Interested on any other request during those 3 hours.

Nusrat calls Rahim, and they agree she will come at 11 AM.

Mitu also sees the alert and taps **I'm Interested**, then gets busy at school and forgets about it. Her story continues in Scene 8.

## Scene 6: The emergency alert

By evening Rahim has one confirmed donor but needs two. He taps **Emergency Alert** on his request. The app warns him he can use it only once every 15 days, and he confirms.

This time the alert goes to **every donor in Dhaka district** with alerts ON, whatever their blood group or status. Even Arif (A+) and Tanvir (Yellow) get it: "Emergency: B+ blood needed in Bashundhara. Can you help or share?"

Arif can't give to a B+ patient, but his cousin Sabbir is B+ and not on Reddify. Arif opens the request, sees Rahim's number and forwards it to Sabbir. Sabbir calls Rahim that night and agrees to donate.

If Rahim tries Emergency Alert again next week, the button is disabled with "Available again in 8 days."

## Scene 7: The request is fulfilled and Nusrat answers

Nusrat tapped I'm Interested on a request needed tomorrow, so the app doesn't ask her anything tonight. Her "Did you donate?" question is scheduled for 8 PM tomorrow, the evening of the required date. Her 3 hours hidden from search end tonight as usual.

The next morning Nusrat and Sabbir both donate at the hospital. Rahim opens **My Requests** and marks the request **Fulfilled**. Everyone who tapped I'm Interested, Nusrat and Mitu, gets a notification: "This request was closed by the seeker." Because it was Fulfilled, not Cancelled, they are also asked: "Did you successfully donate blood?"

Nusrat taps **Yes**. A donation with today's date is added to her history, and her status turns **Yellow**. Her card shows "Available in 112 days", her total donations go up to 2, and she gets no automatic request alerts until she is Green again.

If Nusrat had donated somewhere without using I'm Interested, she could tap **Add donation** on her dashboard to record it herself.

## Scene 8: Other donors' paths

**Mitu never answers.** When Rahim marked the request Fulfilled, she got "Did you donate?" and ignored it. A reminder came later, and she ignored that too. 24 hours after the first question, the app quietly returns her to Green, and she reappears in search. The question stays on her dashboard. If she had actually donated, she could still tap Yes later, and she would become Yellow from that date.

**Karim takes a break.** Exams start next week, and Karim doesn't want calls. He sets his status to **Red**. He disappears from search and gets no request alerts. After exams he switches back to Green himself.

**Tanvir mutes alerts.** Tanvir is still Yellow and finds the notifications distracting, so he turns **Receive blood request alerts** OFF. He stays visible in search, labelled "Available in 44 days". On day 84 after his last donation, the app turns him Green automatically. He can still be found and called, but he won't get request or emergency alerts until he turns them back ON.

## Scene 9: Rahim becomes a donor

A week later, grateful for the help, Rahim decides to register as a donor. On his home screen he still sees **Register as Donor**, because he is only a seeker so far. When he taps it, his name and mobile number are already filled in. He adds only his blood group (A−), gender, date of birth, district and area, and last donation date (never). He accepts the consent message.

He doesn't create a second account. The same login now works for both roles. His dashboard keeps **My Requests**, showing his father's request as Fulfilled, and adds a green donor card. The Register as Donor button disappears from his home screen.

If Rahim ever needs blood again, he just taps Find Blood or Post Request from the same account.

## Rules the story showed

| Scene | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Donors must be 18–60; starting status comes from the last donation date |
| 1 | Exact location and extra address details are never shown |
| 2 | Seekers sign up with name, number and password only |
| 2 | Without an open request, search shows no phone numbers |
| 3 | Requests go live immediately; max 3 open per user; no past dates |
| 3 | Request alerts go only to Green donors with compatible groups in the district, alerts ON |
| 4 | Exact-group donors are listed before compatible ones; GPS adds a distance range |
| 5 | I'm Interested shares the donor's number and hides them from search for 3 hours; "Did you donate?" comes at 8 PM on the required date, or when the request is Fulfilled |
| 6 | Emergency alerts reach all donors in the district, once per seeker every 15 days |
| 7 | Fulfilled or Cancelled closes the request; interested donors are notified |
| 7 | A recorded donation makes the donor Yellow for 84 days (men) or 112 days (women) |
| 8 | No answer to "Did you donate?" restores the donor after 24 hours |
| 8 | Red hides a donor; the alerts switch is separate from availability |
| 9 | One account serves both roles; becoming a donor reuses name and number |

Not shown in the story: a request nobody closes expires 48 hours after its required date.
